**isteam transform syntax:** everything after `/:/` is a render instruction.

- Strip `/:/...` entirely → untransformed original
- Append `/:/rs=w:2000` → 2000px-wide render
- `cr=t:X%,l:Y%,w:W%,h:H%` → crop (top, left, width, height as percentages)
- `rs=w:600,h:451,cg:true` → resize with center-gravity
